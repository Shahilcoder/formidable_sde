## Summary
**lodash** is a JavaScript utility library of battle-tested helpers for arrays, objects, collections, and functions — the "utility belt" you reach for instead of hand-rolling common operations.

## Details
Relevant to the DSA-for-web toolkit: collection helpers (`groupBy`, `keyBy`, `uniq`, `chunk`, `orderBy`), deep operations (`cloneDeep`, `get`, `set`, `merge`), and function wrappers that ship [[Debouncing]] (`_.debounce`) and [[Throttling]] (`_.throttle`) out of the box. `_.keyBy` is essentially the [[Hashmap Lookup Pattern]] as a one-liner.

Docs: <https://lodash.com/docs/4.17.15>
